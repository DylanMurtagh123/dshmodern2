Common questions

Roofing questions, answered

The things homeowners in Ashford ask us most before booking a job in.

How much will my roof cost?

It depends on the size of the roof, the materials used and how much access is involved, so we don't quote blind. We come out, take a proper look and give you a free, no-obligation written quote before any work starts.

Are you fully insured?

Yes. Heron Roofing carries full public liability insurance, so you're covered while we're on your property and on the roof.

How long does a new roof take to fit?

Most domestic re-roofs take between a few days and around a week, depending on the size of the roof, the weather and whether any timber needs replacing underneath. We'll give you a realistic timeframe as part of your quote.

Do you offer free quotes?

Always. We'll come and inspect the roof in person and talk you through what it needs, with no charge and no pressure to go ahead.

What areas do you cover?

We're based in Ashford and cover the surrounding towns and villages across Kent. If you're not sure whether you're in range, get in touch and we'll let you know.

My roof is leaking — how quickly can you come out?

We treat leaks as a priority and aim to get out to look at active leaks as fast as possible, usually within a few days. Call us and let us know it's urgent.

Do you remove and dispose of the old roofing waste?

Yes, all old tiles, felt and waste materials are cleared and disposed of properly as part of the job. We leave your property tidy once we're finished.

Is my new roof guaranteed?

Yes, all of our work is guaranteed. We'll confirm the specific guarantee for your job, and the materials used, as part of your written quote.

Can you work in poor weather?

Roofing is weather-dependent, especially for new roofs and flat roofs. If conditions aren't safe or suitable, we'll reschedule to protect the quality of the work rather than rush it.
