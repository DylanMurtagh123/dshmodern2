Who we are

Design Blinds is an independent, made-to-measure blinds and shutters business based in Braintree, Essex. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us, request a quote, or use this website, and what we do with it.

We are the "data controller" for the purposes of UK data protection law, meaning we're responsible for deciding how and why your personal information is used.

Information we collect

We only collect what we need to give you a quote, arrange a visit, and keep in touch about your order. This typically includes:

  • Your name, address, phone number and email address
  • Details about your windows, rooms and the blinds you're interested in
  • Any messages you send us by phone, email, WhatsApp, or through this website
  • Basic technical information about how you use this website, such as pages visited and general location, gathered anonymously through standard website analytics

We do not knowingly collect information from children, and this website is not directed at them.

How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Arrange and carry out measuring and fitting visits
  • Provide quotes and process orders
  • Respond to enquiries and follow up after a visit or fitting
  • Keep basic records for accounting, warranty and legal purposes

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not sell your personal information to anyone.

WhatsApp & messaging

If you contact us via WhatsApp, phone or email, your message and contact details are handled the same way as any other enquiry. Please note that WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and any message you send is also subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy, which is outside our control.

Cookies

This website may use a small number of essential cookies needed for it to function correctly, and may use basic analytics cookies to help us understand how the site is used. These do not identify you personally. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Storing your information

We take reasonable steps to keep your information secure, including limiting who has access to it. Information is stored securely and only kept for as long as it's needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law (for example, accounting records).

Sharing your information

We don't share your personal information with third parties except where necessary to run our business, such as:

  • Suppliers who manufacture your blinds to the measurements provided
  • Our accountant, for bookkeeping purposes
  • Where we're required to by law

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we're not required to keep it
  • Object to how your information is used

To exercise any of these rights, just get in touch using the details below. If you're not satisfied with our response, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

How long we keep your information

We generally keep customer and order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order, honour any guarantee, and meet our accounting and legal obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if our services or the law change. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
