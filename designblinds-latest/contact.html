What to include in your message.

You do not need exact measurements or a final decision before getting in touch.

01

Your town or postcode

This helps confirm the service area and plan a convenient home visit.

02

The rooms and approximate number of windows

A simple overview is enough—we'll take the accurate measurements in person.

03

Any styles you already like

Wood, Roller, Roman, Vertical or something else; it is equally fine to say you are undecided.

04

What the room needs

Blackout, privacy, glare control, easy cleaning or child-safe operation often points towards the right shortlist.
