Style & Character

All that light, on your terms.

Roof windows flood a room with light — brilliant on a bright morning, less so when you're trying to get back to sleep at 5am in June. Genuine VELUX® blinds give you back control, without losing any of the light a roof window is there for in the first place.

Choose from our standard fabric range for everyday light-filtering and privacy, or step up to VELUX® Blackout Roller Blinds for total darkness, whatever the weather's doing outside. Patterns and plain designs are both available, so it's easy to match the rest of the room.

Blackout is a genuine favourite for children's bedrooms and for shift workers who need to sleep during the day — light through a roof window has a habit of finding its way in no matter how you're feeling about it. The blind itself has a reflective aluminium coating on the reverse, bouncing summer heat back out and helping hold onto warmth in winter.

Because every VELUX® window model is slightly different, we measure and fit against the exact model in your home, so the blind clips in cleanly and sits flush — no gaps, no light creeping in round the edges.
