Local Knowledge

Historic character, modern growth.

Great Dunmow is a historic Essex market town with a recognisable High Street of timber frames, plaster, brick and individual shopfronts. Its conservation area reflects that long history, while newer neighbourhoods continue to expand the town beyond its traditional core.

The famous Dunmow Flitch custom gives the town a story found nowhere else. Associated with a medieval tradition and revived as a civic event in the nineteenth century, the trials remain part of Great Dunmow's identity and community life.

The housing is just as varied. Older properties may have deep or irregular reveals that benefit from a careful individual measure; modern family homes often bring broader panes, glazed doors and rooms where flexible privacy matters. Romans and wood suit character interiors, while rollers, verticals and metal Venetians offer crisp control in newer spaces.

We travel from Braintree along the A120 with the samples, so decisions are made beside your own paint, flooring and daylight. The visit is free, with fitting included when you order.
