Style & Character

The effortless choice.

Roller blinds are the effortless choice — clean, uncluttered lines that add real style to a room without the price tag of a Roman blind. Simple to operate and simple to love, they suit almost any window in the house.

Need proper darkness? Our blackout fabrics are a favourite for children's bedrooms and shift workers alike, keeping the sun, heat and early mornings firmly outside. Prefer to soften the light instead? Our sheer, light-filtering fabrics let daylight drift in while keeping the room private.

It's not just about the fabric, either. Finish your blind with decorative eyelets, a shaped hem, and a choice of poles and finials in chrome, silver, brass or timber — small details that turn a functional blind into something that actually looks considered.

With plains, textures, stripes and geometrics alongside the blackout and sheer ranges — and a number of fabrics suitable for commercial spaces needing flame-retardant or solar-reflective performance — there's a roller blind for pretty much anywhere you need one.
