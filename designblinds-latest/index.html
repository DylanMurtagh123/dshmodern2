Child Safety

Safety you don't have to think about.

Every blind we fit is made with child safety in mind, in line with current British and European safety standards — so you can choose the look you want without compromising on it.

01

Compliant as standard

All our blinds meet current child-safety requirements under BS EN 13120, with cords and chains fitted with the correct safety devices.

02

Cords kept out of reach

Cleats, breakaway connectors and chain tensioners are fitted as standard, keeping loose cords secured safely away from little hands.

03

Cordless options available

For bedrooms, playrooms and nurseries, we can recommend cordless or spring-operated blinds with no hanging cord at all.

04

Advice at every visit

We'll talk you through the safest options for any room, and fit everything with your household in mind.

Still deciding? We're happy to talk you through it — no pressure, no pitch.
