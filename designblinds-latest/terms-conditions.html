Introduction

These terms apply whenever you book a measuring visit, place an order, or have blinds fitted by Design Blinds, based in Braintree, Essex. By booking a visit or placing an order with us, you're agreeing to these terms.

Quotes & measuring visits

We offer a free home visit to measure your windows and provide a quote. Measuring visits are usually arranged within a few days of your call, subject to availability.

Quotes are valid for 30 days from the date given, after which prices may need to be reconfirmed.

Orders & payment

Once you accept a quote, we'll place your order with our suppliers. Because blinds are made to your specific measurements, orders cannot usually be changed once manufacturing has started.

Payment terms (such as any deposit required, and when the balance is due) will be agreed with you at the time of order. We accept the payment methods discussed at your measuring visit.

Lead times vary by product and fabric, but most orders are ready to fit within two to three weeks of ordering. We'll always give you a realistic timeframe when you order.

Fitting

Once your blinds arrive, we'll arrange a convenient time to fit them. Fitting is carried out by the same person who measured your windows, to make sure everything fits as intended.

We'll leave your home as tidy as we found it, and remove any packaging unless you'd prefer to keep it.

Access to your property

To carry out a measuring or fitting visit, we'll need reasonable access to the relevant windows and rooms. If a booked visit needs to be rearranged, please let us know as early as possible.

Cancellations

You can cancel or rearrange a measuring visit at any time before it takes place, free of charge.

Because most blinds are made to measure, orders may not be able to be cancelled once production has begun. If you need to cancel an order, please contact us as soon as possible and we'll let you know if this is possible.

Guarantee & warranty

All blinds come with a manufacturer's guarantee covering faults in materials or workmanship. In addition, we stand behind our own fitting — if something isn't right after installation, get in touch and we'll put it right.

This guarantee doesn't cover damage caused by misuse, accidents, or normal wear and tear over time.

Liability

We take care to carry out measuring and fitting to a high standard. We're not responsible for pre-existing issues with your windows, walls or fixtures that affect fitting, and we'll always raise anything we notice before proceeding.

Nothing in these terms affects your statutory rights as a consumer under UK law.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with any part of our service, please contact us directly so we can put things right. Most issues can be resolved quickly with a phone call or a follow-up visit.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales, and any disputes will be handled by the courts of England and Wales.
