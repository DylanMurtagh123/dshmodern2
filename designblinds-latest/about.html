Our Story

Built locally, one recommendation at a time.

Design Blinds has spent more than two decades measuring windows and fitting blinds around Braintree, Great Dunmow, Chelmsford, Witham and the towns and villages between them. The business has grown through professional work, straightforward advice and customers being happy to recommend the service.

Remaining independent matters. There is no national call centre, showroom sales target or hand-off between a salesperson and an unknown fitter. We visit your home, listen to what the room needs, measure the windows and return to install the finished blinds.

The blinds are made to measure here in the UK. That allows the range to combine dependable quality with plenty of choice: natural timber, moisture-resistant faux wood, crisp aluminium, blackout rollers, soft Romans, practical verticals and purpose-made roof-window blinds.

A new blind can do more than cover glass. Fabric can soften a room, a slat colour can sharpen it, and the right level of light filtering can change how the space feels throughout the day. The aim is to find that balance without making the decision complicated—or the price unreasonable.
