Style & Character

The tailored choice.

There's a reason interior designers reach for Roman blinds first. Raised, the fabric stacks into neat, even folds across the top of the window; lowered, it sits as one clean panel of pattern or texture — structured, tailored, and unmistakably considered.

Because the whole blind is fabric, they're the best way to bring a serious print or a beautiful weave into a room. Stripes, checks, botanicals, plains and heavier linens all work — the fold itself becomes part of the design, catching the light a little differently, line by line.

Most of our Romans are lined, which adds a softer drape, better insulation against the cold, and a more settled hang. Ask about a blackout lining for bedrooms, or a thermal lining to help keep the heat in through winter and out in summer.

They're not just for show, either — smooth chain or cord operation raises and lowers the blind evenly, fold by fold, and every one is made to the exact width and drop of your window, so it hangs properly from day one.
