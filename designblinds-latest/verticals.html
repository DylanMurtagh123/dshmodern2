Style & Character

Curtains, without the compromise.

Available in a vast range of colours, textures and finishes, our vertical blinds are as at home in a bright, modern kitchen or conservatory as they are softening a bedroom or lounge with a gentle pastel, jacquard or textured fabric. Whatever the room, there's a shade and finish to complement your furniture and décor.

Many of our vertical fabrics are also available across our roller blind range, so you can carry the same shade or texture from room to room — or through into a home office — for a properly considered, uniform look across the whole house.

Think of them as a smarter, more affordable alternative to curtains. They offer similar light-excluding ability, but the individual louvres tilt to filter natural daylight in and cut glare without shutting the room off completely — combining everyday function with a clean, tailored look and genuine control over the sun.

For rooms that need to stay properly dark, our blackout verticals come in a wide choice of plain colours, alongside a select range of patterned blackout fabrics — plus sun-dimming and flame-retardant options wherever you need them.
