Style & Character

A style that never goes out of fashion.

Our made-to-measure wood Venetian blinds are full of style and colour, with options to suit every window and room. Choose from a large selection of exciting wood colours — from whites and creams to big, bold colours, natural wood tones and darker modern shades. Wood Venetians offer all the benefits of a Venetian blind, with excellent light control and privacy, plus the lovely warmth that natural wood brings to your home.

Both stylish and functional, wood Venetians create a real sense of style in any room — living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens and family rooms alike. Quality woods, from rich dark hardwoods to lovely tones like pecan, cedar and hickory, give our blinds real character, with a natural warmth that only wood can bring.

It's a sophisticated look that works in both traditional and modern homes — a design that never goes out of fashion, adding warmth and personality to any décor, from trendy piano gloss black and chic white through to softer, natural wood tones. Choose from four slat widths and a range of tape colours, and finish it with a deluxe ladder tape for an elegant contrasting or coordinating look, or keep it simple with a classic cord design. They'll become a real focal point of the room — that shutter look, without the high cost of shutters.

Easy to operate and highly flexible, our wood Venetians let you set exactly the level of privacy and light you want. Closed, they offer complete privacy; opened, you're in control of the natural light flooding into the room — all with the warmth and style only natural wood can bring. Never out of style, they're both a fashionable choice and a timeless classic.

FAQs

Wooden Venetians, answered.

The questions homeowners usually ask before choosing wooden Venetians. Can't see yours? Get in touch and we'll answer it directly.

Real wood isn't the best choice for very steamy bathrooms, since moisture can cause the slats to warp over time. For bathrooms and other high-moisture rooms we'd usually recommend our Faux Wood Venetians instead, which give the same look with much better moisture resistance.

35mm and 50mm slats suit most windows and give a classic look, while 63mm slats work well on larger windows and bring a bolder, more contemporary feel. We'll bring samples to your home visit so you can see how each width looks in your own light.

A regular dust with a soft cloth or the brush attachment on a vacuum keeps them looking their best. Avoid soaking the slats in water — a lightly damp cloth is fine for the occasional deeper clean.

Yes. Every blind comes with a child safety device that the cord wraps safely around, fitted free of charge as standard. For bedrooms and nurseries we can also fit cordless or spring-operated versions with no hanging cord at all.

Most wooden Venetian orders are made and fitted within two to three weeks of your free measuring visit, depending on the finish and slat width you choose.
