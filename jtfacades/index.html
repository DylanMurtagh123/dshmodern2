Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about external wall insulation and render. Can't find your answer? Give John a call.

External wall insulation (EWI) involves fixing insulation boards directly to the outside of your existing walls, then covering them with a reinforced base coat and a decorative render finish. It creates a continuous thermal envelope around your home, eliminating cold bridges and dramatically reducing heat loss — without any disruption to the inside of your property.
Most homeowners see energy bill savings of between 20–40% after having external wall insulation installed. The exact saving depends on your property type, wall construction, and current heating usage — but you'll typically feel a noticeable difference in warmth from the very first winter. Your home's EPC rating will also improve, which can increase its market value.
We offer an extensive range of colours across our silicone, monocouche, and stone render systems. From classic whites and creams through to bold contemporary greys, warm earth tones, and everything in between — there's a shade to complement any property style. We'll bring colour samples to your free survey so you can see exactly how they'll look on your home.
For a typical semi-detached or detached property, the full installation — including scaffolding, insulation, base coat, and render finish — usually takes between 5 and 10 working days depending on the size of the property and the complexity of the elevation. We'll give you a clear timeline before work begins and keep you updated throughout.
Almost none. Because EWI is an external system, all work is carried out from the outside. You can continue living in your home as normal throughout the installation. The only minor considerations are that windows and doors may temporarily have a slightly reduced opening while scaffolding is in place, and there will be some noise during working hours.
Our silicone renders carry a 25-year product warranty and are designed to be self-cleaning — meaning rainwater washes dirt away naturally, keeping your home looking fresh for years. The insulation system itself, when properly installed, has a lifespan of 30+ years. We use only premium, industry-approved products to ensure lasting results.
In most cases, external wall insulation falls under permitted development and does not require planning permission. However, if your property is listed, in a conservation area, or subject to specific conditions, restrictions may apply. We'll advise you on this during your free survey — and if permission is needed, we can guide you through the process.
