COMCRAFT CLEANING & FACILITIES — HOMEPAGE DEMO

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html to open the website in your browser.
3. No installation or coding is required.
4. Upload all files and the assets folder together to GitHub Pages or your hosting provider if you want to show it via a link.

CONTENTS
- index.html: website structure and content
- styles.css: desktop/mobile styling
- script.js: menu and demo-form behaviour
- assets/: local image assets derived from initial design mockups

IMPORTANT BEFORE LAUNCH
- Concept imagery is illustrative and should be replaced by approved client photos.
- No real client reviews, phone numbers, emails or unsupported experience figures are included.
- The enquiry form is intentionally DEMO ONLY; it does not send messages.
- Connect the final form to FormSubmit or the customer's preferred provider before launch.
- Confirm services, service areas, company details, policies, and business claims.
- Google Fonts are loaded online; the site falls back to system fonts offline.
